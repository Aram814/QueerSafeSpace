🆘 Crisis Resources

You are not alone. Help is available 24/7.

Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ Youth)

1-866-488-7386

24/7 crisis support for LGBTQ+ young people under 25. Also available via chat & text.

Trans Lifeline

1-877-565-8860

Trans-led peer support hotline staffed by and for transgender people.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

988

Free, confidential 24/7 support. Press 3 for dedicated LGBTQ+ support.

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741

Free 24/7 text-based support from trained crisis counselors.

GLBT National Hotline

1-888-843-4564

Peer support, information and local resources for LGBTQ+ people of all ages.

National DV Hotline (LGBTQ+ inclusive)

1-800-799-7233

Safe, confidential support for anyone experiencing domestic violence.
